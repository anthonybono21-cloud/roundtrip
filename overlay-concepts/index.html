Why start with Place first?

The location leads without spreading your attention across the screen. The larger logo feels intentional beside the information; the temperature and time keep their prominence. Wide rail suits a desk display. Open corners gives each element room, but asks your eyes to travel farther.

Place first is the selected production layout, with a static logo. Label examples use the existing camera record. In the app, state/province can sit between city and country when available; keep the camera name on the second line.
